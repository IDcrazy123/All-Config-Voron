CARTOGRAPHER_SCAN_MODEL: Manage saved scan models
CARTOGRAPHER_SCAN_PROBE: Probe the bed to get the height offset at the current position.

CARTOGRAPHER_TOUCH_CALIBRATE: Run the touch calibration
CARTOGRAPHER_TOUCH_HOME: Touch the bed to home Z axis
CARTOGRAPHER_TOUCH_MODEL: Manage saved touch models
CARTOGRAPHER_TOUCH_PROBE: Touch the bed to get the height offset at the current position.
